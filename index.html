Questions

Before we talk.

Which industries do you work in?

Any. The engineering is the same whether the product serves finance, HR, healthcare, logistics or retail. I learn your domain early in the discovery step and ask for the people who know it best.

Do you offer security reviews, testing and AI development?

Yes, as part of a project or on their own: secure code review, API security testing, test automation, and AI features such as chatbots and document processing. Tell me what you need and I will scope it in writing first.

How do you price projects?

Most projects are fixed price against a written scope, or a capped estimate for work that is harder to define. For ongoing support I can work hourly. You get the numbers before anything starts.

How soon can you start, and how much time can you commit?

I take on a limited number of projects at a time so each one gets proper attention. Tell me your timeline in the first message and I'll tell you honestly whether it fits.

Can you take over an existing .NET application?

Yes. I start with a short review of the code, database and deployment, then give you a plan covering fixes, upgrades and risks.

Which time zones can you work with?

I'm based in India (IST) and regularly work with clients in the Gulf and Europe. For US teams we agree a fixed overlap window.

Who owns the code?

You do. Source code, documentation and credentials are handed over at the end of the project, and the work is covered by an NDA if you need one.
